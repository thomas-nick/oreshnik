## What this is

A personal site for design notes, half-finished projects, and the occasional
travelogue. Built on the [Brutal](https://brutal.elian.codes/) Astro theme,
edited through [Sveltia CMS](https://sveltia.com/) at `/admin`, and broadcast
to [mahoot.xyz](https://mahoot.xyz).

The aesthetic is **sovietwave** — not because of any politics, but because the
visual language of mid-century Soviet space posters happens to overlap nicely
with neobrutalism: bold type, limited palette, geometric stars, and an
unapologetic willingness to take up space.

## What's coming

- **Design notes** — small UX problems, screenshots, before/afters
- **Project write-ups** — what I built, what worked, what didn't
- **Travelogue** — Bangkok, Northern Thailand, sometimes elsewhere

## How to follow

- [RSS feed](/feed.xml)
- Or just check back. There's no algorithm here, no newsletter form yet, and
  no notifications. The signal is faint by design.
